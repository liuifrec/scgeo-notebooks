# Results-first public applications

Explore actual ScGeo 0.2.1 biological outputs without downloading the original data or installing ScGeo. The figures are committed, deterministic visualizations from compact numerical snapshots; the notebooks recalculate summaries from those snapshots.

| Dataset | Design and inference scope | Result notebook | Illustrated gallery |
|---|---|---|---|
| GSE249479 — human HSPC inflammation | 34,432 retained cells, 14 pooled state-contrast explanations; biological replicate identities unavailable | [TNF versus LPS](gse249479/GSE249479_inflammation_results.ipynb) | [Five figures](gse249479/README.md) |
| GSE211713 — mouse-lung radiation | 131,157 retained cells, 27 state-contrast explanations; three cross-sectional contrasts of 13 qualified primary-roster mice | [Fibroblast responses](gse211713/GSE211713_fibroblast_results.ipynb) | [Six figures](gse211713/README.md) |

Both analyses use their verified original linear representation. Neither gallery represents cell trajectories, causal regulation or independently validated predictors. Retrospectively selected molecular examples are not confirmatory tests. The complete [geometry and molecular analysis notebooks](../v0.2.0/README.md) are separately reproducible with the original inputs. The compact results-first notebooks are designed for immediate browsing and lightweight execution.
