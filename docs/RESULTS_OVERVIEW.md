# Frozen results overview

This page is a navigation summary, not a new analysis. Exact values belong in
the frozen evidence ledgers and figure-source tables.

## Synthetic benchmark

The benchmark tests controlled ground truth with calibration and held-out
evaluation separated. Accepted negative findings remain visible, including
balanced-replicate seed dependence, incomplete state-level instability recall,
limited local-distortion recall in explicit corruption jobs, and imperfect
bootstrap coverage. These results support method-behavior claims only.

## Public pancreas

The workflow compares representation geometry with public developmental
dynamics. scVelo and CellRank provide related context; CellRank is not treated
as independent evidence from RNA velocity. The conclusions are descriptive.

## Dataset B, GSE249479

The compact analysis retained 34,432 cells. No valid biological-replicate
identity was recoverable, so every result is `descriptive_only`. Frozen evidence
includes stable descriptive TNF shifts in Activated HSC and Lymphoid, broader
LPS displacement, an abundance-dominant LPS HSC/quiescent pattern, high primary
representation rank agreement, and diffusion-map sensitivity with limited
influence on primary conclusions. Official R Augur is the main comparator; the
Python approximation is supplementary implementation sensitivity.

## Dataset C, GSE211713

The analysis retained 131,157 cells from 20 independent mouse libraries.
Primary comparisons are replicate-aware and cross-sectional. Under the frozen
consensus rule, early 17 Gy remodeling was representation-sensitive; late 17 Gy
showed stable associations in five of six major compartments; and all six major
compartments differed stably between early and late irradiated mice. The
Fibroblast/stromal major state and fibroblast subtypes showed greater
representation sensitivity. These associations do not establish longitudinal
change or causality.
